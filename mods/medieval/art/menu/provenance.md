# Illustration de l’accueil

- Fichier : `bourg-dusk.png`.
- Création : 10 octobre 2026, outil de génération d’images intégré `image_gen` (aucun appel API payant externe).
- Destination : chargement et accueil des Terres du Bourg.
- L’original est conservé sans retouche. Le moteur place ses pixels dans une texture compatible, puis adapte l’affichage à la fenêtre en conservant les proportions. Le titre et les boutons sont rendus séparément.
- Les panneaux de l’interface (`panels.png`) sont des éléments géométriques produits par `tools/frontend_content.py`.

## Prompt utilisé

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: final production loading-screen and main-menu background for the existing medieval real-time strategy game 'Les Terres du Bourg'. Create an expansive 16:9 landscape illustration, high quality, cinematic painted 1990s strategy-game box-art spirit, grounded late-medieval European village economy. View from a gently elevated hillside across an inhabited timber-and-plaster village: market with muted colored awnings, timber-framed houses, golden crop fields, cow and sheep enclosures, small lumber mill, stone quarry and blacksmith smoke. A supply cart on a winding dirt road crosses a stone bridge over a river. A few tiny militia, a drummer and a standard bearer near a wooden palisade, a small timber trebuchet being prepared in the far middle ground. Woodland, meadows, distant hills. Late golden afternoon turning toward dusk, warm hearth lights against forest greens and slate blue mountains, warm ochre and restrained burgundy accents. Believable coherent architectural perspective, detailed but readable and composed, atmospheric depth, high craft. The left quarter and upper left should be dark quiet forest shadow with low detail and broad tonal continuity, reserved for a vertical menu and title that the game will render separately. Main village focal point in middle/right. No text, no lettering, no logos, no UI, no frames, no watermark. No modern technology, no tanks, no guns, no fantasy creatures, no exaggerated cathedral or royal castle dominating this village game. Opaque full-bleed background.
