# Sources isométriques — 7 octobre 2026

Deux planches produites avec le générateur d’images intégré à Codex, puis copiées dans ce dossier. Aucun appel réseau n’est nécessaire pour reconstruire le mod.

- `buildings-isometric.png` : sept bâtiments. Référence géométrique produite par `../tools/building-projection-guide.py`, référence de matériaux `medieval-atlas.png`. La huitième cellule est une référence de chantier ; les chantiers utilisés en jeu sont dessinés par code pour correspondre exactement à chaque empreinte.
- `nature-isometric.png` : chêne, conifère, bouleau, pierre, buisson, herbes, fleurs et souche. La planche de bâtiments sert de référence artistique.
- `../tools/isometric_art.py` : compilation des PNG par découpe, rééchantillonnage et assemblage ; ancrages des fondations explicites, échelle commune des bâtiments. Sols, sillons, clôtures et chantiers suivent une projection calculée `(x-y)*24, (x+y)*12`. Les objets verticaux sont dessinés après projection de leur point d’appui.

## Prompt des bâtiments

Repaint IMAGE 1, the geometric isometric building atlas, into finished medieval RTS pixel-art sprites. IMAGE 1 IS THE GEOMETRY AND COMPOSITION AUTHORITY: preserve exactly its 4-column x 2-row layout, each building's position, scale, ground contact corners, straight verticals, parallel edges, roof planes and 2:1 isometric camera. Its two horizontal ground axes are (+2,+1) and (-2,+1). The dark blue rectangles indicate front doors. Do not adopt the different camera from image 2. IMAGE 2 is ONLY a materials, detail and identity reference: weathered limestone, timber framing, roofing shingles, terracotta, thatch, modest blue cloth accents, sawmill tools, barrels, produce and small tightly grouped props. Render the following eight objects in the positions shown by IMAGE 1: cottage; stone warehouse; open timber sawmill; stonemason workshop with upright crane; windmill with grain barn; cattle barn; market pavilion with blue-white awnings; unfinished building with low foundation and scaffold. All seven finished buildings and the scaffold must look as if rendered by ONE fixed orthographic camera. No converging edges, no lens perspective, no stretched right side, no skewed roof. Roof support corners must meet their supporting walls. Keep the foundation corners exactly where they are in the geometric guide, and maintain the cottage's smaller scale. Add rich crisp authentic 1990s RTS pixel texture, individual roof tiles, stone joints, timber braces and well-shaped doors/windows ON the existing planes. Upgrade the primitive windmill to a round stone tower with properly projected sails, preserving its location, height and base. Remove the large flat ground diamonds from the guide: output only architecture and tight contact shadows, no platform border or terrain tile. Finish all surfaces with the reference's detailed warm medieval material style, NOT flat polygons or low-poly art. Consistent illumination from upper left, brighter left-front facade and darker right-front facade, very short translucent shadows cast to the lower right. Preserve generous transparent gutters and do not enlarge any object to fill empty cell space. Transparent alpha background. No text, labels, people or extra objects. Output wide 2:1 atlas, same composition and scale as IMAGE 1.

## Prompt de la végétation

Create one transparent medieval RTS nature sprite atlas, 4 columns x 2 rows with eight isolated objects. Use the supplied building atlas solely as the art style and lighting reference. All nature sprites must use the same orthographic 2:1 isometric view as these buildings: see the top of the canopy, vertical trunks, ground axes slope +1/2 and -1/2. Crisp detailed 1990s strategy-game pixel art, earthy muted greens, amber highlights, fine clustered leaf texture, rugged bark, upper-left lighting with subdued right side. Top row left to right: a broad mature oak tree, a tall conifer pine tree, a slender birch tree with two white trunks, a small quarry outcrop of angular pale-grey stone. Bottom row left to right: a dense low wild bramble bush, a group of wild grasses with a few tan reeds, a small cluster of grass and tiny cream wildflowers, one cut oak stump with exposed roots. No platforms or flat ground diamonds. Compact soft contact shadows extending very slightly lower right, fully transparent background. Each isolated object centered inside its own cell with broad transparent gutters and no overlap. Trees in top row occupy about 80 percent cell height and rocks 40 percent cell height, all ground contacts at 88 percent of their cell height; bottom row shrubs, grasses and stump small, maximum 35 percent cell height with the same ground contact baseline. Thick trees look dimensional, volumes overhang their trunks, no spherical single blob canopies. No text, labels, people, buildings, extra objects or checkerboard. Output a wide 2:1 atlas.
