# Graphismes — Les Terres du Bourg

`medieval-atlas.png` a été créé le 7 octobre 2026 avec le générateur d’images intégré à Codex. La planche est une création pour ce mod, sans extraction des sprites commerciaux d’Alerte Rouge. Elle contient sept bâtiments, un chêne et un gisement. Son fond transparent est conservé lors de l’importation.

Le générateur `../tools/generate-content.py` importe ces neuf cellules et crée les planches PNG du moteur. Les cadres, animations directionnelles, cultures, clôtures, bovins, ressources et terrains sont des graphismes éditables définis dans `../tools/medieval_art.py`. La génération reste reproductible hors ligne ; elle ne rappelle pas le service d’images.

## Prompt utilisé (générateur intégré, une planche)

Create ONE production-ready transparent PNG sprite atlas for a medieval total conversion of a classic 1995 Command & Conquer Red Alert style RTS. Square atlas, EXACTLY 3 columns and 3 rows of equally sized cells. Nine isolated objects, one centered in each cell with generous transparent gutters so they can be cut apart automatically. NO text, NO labels, NO panel borders, NO backdrop, NO ground diamond or terrain base. Orthographic camera looking down at approximately 55 degrees, north at top; rectangular building fronts horizontal, a little east side visible. Consistent light from upper left, small tight semitransparent shadow to lower right. Very detailed hand-painted pixel art, crisp pixel clusters, realistic restrained proportions, weathered limestone and dark oak, tiled roofs, muted earthy colors, sharp readable silhouettes at 80 pixel game scale; emulate the grounded pre-rendered pixel sprites of mid-1990s RTS, NOT cartoon, NOT voxel, NOT modern smooth 3D, NOT high fantasy. Limited shades per material, strong dark occlusion beneath eaves. Small cobalt blue cloth or painted door accents identify friendly buildings. Each object occupies at most 78 percent of its cell and never touches any neighbor. Row 1 left: small half-timber medieval cottage with steep dark terracotta gabled roof, masonry chimney, blue door and two windows. Row 1 center: large stone and timber warehouse with broad slate roof, large dark loading doorway, stacks of barrels and square crates against the facade. Row 1 right: open-sided timber sawmill workshop with a wood shingle roof, visible saw bench, trestles, neatly piled cut logs and a large vertical saw wheel. Row 2 left: stonemason workshop with low stone walls, grey shingle roof over one half, wooden lifting crane, chiseled limestone blocks and workbench. Row 2 center: grain farm with a distinctive small cylindrical stone windmill on the left, four clear wooden sail blades, low attached thatched barn, sacks of wheat at doorway. Row 2 right: cattle ranch building, long dark red timber barn with straw roof, open double doors, lean-to shelter, trough and hay bales. Row 3 left: medieval marketplace with a central open wooden pavilion and two striped blue and cream canvas stalls, baskets of produce and hanging pennant. Row 3 center: lush mature oak tree with individually shaded foliage clusters, thick branching trunk, realistic full crown, believable tiny pixel texture, no round cartoon circles. Row 3 right: compact rugged limestone outcrop consisting of several angular boulders, layered cracked faces, cool blue-grey shadows and pale sunlit edges. This is a GAME SPRITE ATLAS, all nine objects fully visible, genuine transparent alpha background.
